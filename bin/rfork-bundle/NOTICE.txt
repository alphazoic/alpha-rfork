rfork: https://github.com/mega-fork/rfork

This bundle contains rfork v1.75.2-rfork.0.1.0-alpha.1, a fork of rclone
1.75.2 with concurrent native MEGA chunk uploads and HTTPS enabled by default.
M3 retains the internal provider ID "rclone" for saved-settings compatibility.

LICENSE-rclone.txt contains the upstream rclone copyright and MIT license.
LICENSE-go-mega.txt contains the modified go-mega dependency's MIT license.
manifest.json records each executable's source reference, checksum, validation
evidence, and the M3 revision containing the previous stock rclone bundle.

Only Linux x64, Windows x64 and Intel macOS x64 artifacts are included.
